1. What the app is

Grow Spark Sales & CRM is a web app for the sales team. It covers the whole journey from first form submission to won deal:

form submission → lead → company and contact → deal → quotation → Won or Lost → reports.

It runs in a normal web browser. Grow Spark Consulting is the first organisation using it. Amounts are in Indian rupees (₹) and dates use India time.

There are two roles. Both do everyday sales work. Only Admins manage people and settings.

AdminStaff (shown as "Staff Executive")
Leads, companies, contacts, deals, activitiesYesYes
Quotations, form submissions, reports, CSV export and importYesYes
Home dashboard, Today, My Tasks, search, notificationsYesYes
My profile, change own passwordYesYes
Users (add, edit, deactivate, reset passwords)YesNo
Organisation profile, lead sources, pipeline stagesYesNo
API keys, Audit logYesNo

Staff can see every record, not only their own. "Owner" shows who is responsible for a record. It does not hide it from others.

3. Signing in, users and passwords

Signing in

  1. Open the app's web address.
  2. Type your Email and Password, click Sign in.
  3. A wrong password shows a general error. It never says whether the email exists. After several failed attempts the app asks you to wait.

To sign out, click your name (top right) → Log out. A session lasts up to 7 days. It is extended while you keep using the app.

New users (Admin only)

There is no public sign-up and no email invitation. An Admin creates each account:

  1. Menu → Users → New user.
  2. Enter Name, Email, Role (Admin or Staff) and an Initial password (at least 10 characters). Click Create user.
  3. Give the person their password yourself, in person or by phone.

On the Users list an Admin can also click Edit (name, email, role), Reset password, and Deactivate / Reactivate. A deactivated person is signed out at once. The app will not let you remove or demote the last active Admin.

Changing your own password

  1. Menu → Settings → My profile.
  2. Under Change password, enter Current password, New password and New password again. Click Change password.
  3. You stay signed in here. You are signed out on every other device.

Forgot your password? Ask an Admin to use Reset password on the Users page. There is no "forgot password" email.

4. Home, Today and My Tasks

Home (the dashboard)

  • Nine numbers: Leads created, Qualified leads, Active leads, Open deals, Open pipeline value, Weighted value, Won revenue, Win rate, Overdue tasks.
  • Four charts: Leads by source, Leads by status, Pipeline by stage, Monthly won revenue. Plus a Recent activity list.
  • Date range: This month, Last 30 days or Custom range.
  • All / Mine: everyone's records, or only the ones you own or are assigned.

Open deals and pipeline value always show "right now". They ignore the date range.

Today

One card per question: Overdue, Tasks due today, Meetings this week, New leads, Deals closing soon (next 14 days), Deals with no next activity, Recently updated deals.

  • Mine (default) or All at the top.
  • Click a row to open the record. Click Complete to tick off a task. Click Add follow-up to schedule the next step.

My Tasks

  • Tabs: Open, Overdue, Done.
  • Assigned to: Assigned to me (the default), Everyone, or one colleague by name. When the list is not only yours, an Assigned to column shows whose each item is ("You", a name, or "Unassigned").
  • Filters: Any type, Any priority, Due from, Due to.
  • Complete or Cancel an item from the list.

The choice stays in the web address, so a refresh or a shared link keeps it. A new activity is assigned to the person who creates it unless they pick someone else.

Adding activities (all record pages)

Every lead, company, contact and deal page has a Timeline tab with an Add activity box:

  1. Pick Task, Call, Meeting or Note.
  2. Type a Subject. Add a due date (or start and end times for a meeting), Priority, Assignee and Description if useful.
  3. Click Save activity (or Save note).

An activity added to a deal also shows on its company's and contacts' timelines.

5. Leads

A lead is a possible customer who has not yet been qualified.

Add a lead

  1. Menu → Leads → Add lead.
  2. Fill in the required fields: First name *, Company name *, and an Email or a Phone (one of the two; until one is filled the form says "Provide an email or a phone number.").
  3. Everything else is optional: last name, website, job title, industry, Source, Priority, Owner, budget, timeline, Next follow-up, Requirement, Notes. Source starts as Not specified (saved as Manual).
  4. Click Add lead.

Typing a phone number (the same box on leads, contacts, companies and Settings → Organisation)

  • Pick the country on the left. India (+91) is the default.
  • Type the number without the leading 0. Only digits are accepted.
  • An Indian number has exactly 10 digits (mobiles and landlines with their area code). Once the box is full, extra digits can't be typed, and a short note appears under the box, for example "India (+91) numbers have at most 10 digits."
  • Pasting "+91 98400 12345" or "098400 12345" is tidied up automatically. A pasted number that is too long is refused rather than cut short.
  • A number that is too short shows a message such as "Enter all 10 digits of the number for India (+91)".

If the email, phone or company name matches an existing lead, a yellow "This might already exist." box appears with a link. It is a warning only. You can still save.

Edit, owner and status

  • Click Edit, change fields (including Owner), click Save changes. The Requirement cannot be changed after creation, so the original enquiry is kept. An older lead saved without a company name asks for one when you edit it.
  • On the Overview tab, under Change status, click New, Contacted, Qualified or Unqualified. The current status is highlighted with a tick. While saving, the clicked button shows a spinner and all the buttons are locked; then a message "Status changed to …" appears. The same feedback is used for quotation, deal, task and form submission status buttons.
  • The History tab lists every status change, who made it and when.

Convert a lead

Only a Qualified lead can be converted. Otherwise the Convert button is greyed out ("Qualify the lead first").

  1. Click Convert.
  2. Company: Link existing (suggested if one matches) or Create new.
  3. Contact: Link existing or Create new (made from the lead's name, email and phone).
  4. Deal: leave Create a deal on to start a deal in the first pipeline stage. Adjust Deal name, Value (₹) and Expected close date if needed.
  5. Click Convert lead. Links to the new or linked records appear.

The lead stays in the system with status Converted, its history and a Converted panel linking to what it became.

Archive, filters, export and import

  • Archive hides a lead from the list. Nothing is permanently deleted. Switch the last filter to Archived to see it, then click Restore.
  • Search box plus filters: Any status, Any source, Any priority, Any owner, a created-date range. Click a column heading to sort. Filters stay in the web address, so a filtered view can be bookmarked.
  • Download CSV exports exactly what the current filters show.
  • There are no bulk actions (for example, selecting several leads to reassign them at once). This was agreed for this version.

Import leads from a CSV file

  1. Leads → Import CSV. Optional: Download a sample file.
  2. Click Choose CSV file (CSV only, up to 1 MB and 1,000 rows). From Excel, use File → Save As → CSV UTF-8.
  3. Match the columns: the app guesses each one. Change any column, or set it to Don't import. Unused columns are still saved on the lead as "Other captured answers". Each row needs a first name, a company and an email or phone, so one column must be matched to Company.
  4. Click Check rows. Every row is marked Ready, Duplicate (same email as an existing lead, or earlier in the file) or Error (with the reason).
  5. Click Import N leads. You can download the skipped rows with their reasons.

6. Companies and Contacts

Companies

  1. Menu → Companies → Add company. Only Company name is required. Click More details for legal name, size, revenue, address and description.
  2. Click Add company.

A company page has tabs: Overview, Contacts, Deals, Quotations, Timeline. You can add a contact, deal or quotation straight from its tab.

Contacts

  1. Menu → Contacts → Add contact (a pop-up form).
  2. Required: First name *, Company * and an email or phone.
  3. Click the Company box: it lists the first 20 companies A–Z straight away. Type to narrow the list, then click one (or use the arrow keys and Enter).
  4. Click Add contact.

A contact's company can be changed later but not removed. Every company picker in the app (deals, quotations, converting a lead) and the contact picker on quotations and deals work the same way.

A contact page has Overview, Deals and Timeline tabs. Click Edit to change it.

Both lists have search, an owner filter, Archive / Restore and Download CSV. Contacts also has Import CSV; every row needs a company. A company name in the file that doesn't exist yet is created once, and the preview lists it first.

7. Deals and the pipeline

A deal is a sales opportunity with a value.

Add a deal: Menu → Deals → Add deal. Enter Deal name, Company, Stage, Value, Owner and expected close date. Click Add deal.

List and Board

  • List: a table with search and filters (stage, status, owner, and an Any company box you can type in) and Download CSV.
  • Board: one column per open stage, with totals. Move a card by dragging it, or with its Move to… button.

On a deal page

  • Key facts: Stage, Value, Probability, Weighted value (value × probability), Expected close, Owner, Next activity.
  • Move stage: pick another open stage. Each move is recorded on the Stage history tab.
  • Contacts tab: Add contact, Make primary, remove.
  • Close as Won: probability becomes 100%.
  • Close as Lost: you must pick a Lost reason (Price, Competitor, No budget, No decision, Timing, Not a fit, Unresponsive, Other) and can add a note. Probability becomes 0%.
  • A closed deal is read-only. Reopen sends it back to the first open stage.
  • Archive / Restore work on open or closed deals.

The stages and their probabilities are set in Settings → Pipeline stages (Admin only). There is one pipeline.

8. Form submissions inbox and the website form

How the website form feeds the CRM

The Grow Spark website's contact form is connected (live since 25 November 2026). Every form submission is forwarded to the CRM, using an API key. This is in addition to the website's own saving, emails and HubSpot flow, which are unchanged. If the CRM is asleep or down, the website still works.

When a form submission arrives, the CRM:

  • stores it exactly as received,
  • creates a lead with source Website, unless it has no email or phone (then it is kept as Rejected, with the reason). A website lead does not need a company name,
  • quietly marks obvious bot submissions as Spam,
  • ignores a repeat of the same submission, so retries never create duplicates,
  • notifies every active user.

Using the inbox

  1. Menu → Form submissions. Filter by status (Received, Lead created, Rejected, Spam), form type or date.
  2. Open a submission. Overview shows the details and a link to its lead. Raw payload shows exactly what the website sent.
  3. Mark as spam: also archives the lead it created.
  4. Archive hides it. Restore brings it back.

There is no read/unread marker; use the status and Archive instead. This was agreed for this version.

9. Quotations

Create a quotation

  1. Menu → Quotations → New quotation (or from a company's or deal's Quotations tab).
  2. Choose Company, then Contact (only that company's people), and optionally a Deal. Each box lists its options as soon as you click it.
  3. Issue date defaults to today. Valid until defaults to 30 days later.
  4. Add Line items: Description, Qty, Unit price (₹), optional Disc. % and a GST % for each line (default 18; type any rate from 0 to 100, for example 0, 5, 12, 18, 28 or 0.25; 0 means no GST). Click Add line for more. There is no discount or GST for the whole quotation any more: discount and GST are set line by line.
  5. Next to the totals, choose Prices are: Tax exclusive (default: GST is added on top of the prices you type) or Tax inclusive (the prices you type already include GST, and the GST is worked out from them). Add Terms and Notes if needed.
  6. The totals box updates as you type: Subtotal (before GST), one GST row for each rate used (for example "GST 5%" and "GST 12.5%"), and Total. Each line's Amount is what that line costs the customer. Click Create quotation.

Each quotation gets a number like Q-2026-0001. The numbers restart each year.

PDF: click Download PDF. It shows your organisation's details (from Settings → Organisation), the customer, the lines (with each line's GST %) and the totals with one GST row per rate. When prices include GST it says "Prices are inclusive of GST". Each edit makes a new version (…-v1, …-v2). PDFs downloaded earlier are not changed.

Example: 1 × ₹1,000 at 18%. Tax exclusive: GST ₹180, total ₹1,180. Tax inclusive: ₹847.46 + GST ₹152.54 = ₹1,000.

Quotations made before this change kept their totals exactly: each line got its quotation's old GST rate, and an old quotation-wide discount was folded into every line's discount.

Statuses

  • The current status is the highlighted button under Change status (top of the page). Only the allowed next steps appear beside it. While a change is saving, a small spinner shows and the buttons are locked so it cannot be sent twice; a message "Status changed to …" confirms it.
  • Draft → click Mark as sent → Sent. A draft whose Valid until date has passed can't be sent until you change the date.
  • Sent → Accepted or Rejected. Both are final.
  • Expired: a Sent quotation past its Valid until date becomes Expired automatically.
  • When you accept a quotation linked to an open deal, the app asks "Mark the deal as Won?". Choose Mark deal as Won or Not now.

Only drafts can be edited. Duplicate makes a new draft copy with a new number (useful after a rejection). Archive / Restore as elsewhere.

10. Notifications

The bell (top right) shows how many unread notifications you have. It checks about once a minute. Click it to open Notifications: All or Unread, Mark read on one row, or Mark all as read. Click a title to open the record.

You are notified when:

  • a lead or deal is assigned to you by someone else,
  • a website form submission arrives (everyone is told),
  • a quotation you own is accepted or rejected by someone else,
  • a task assigned to you is due today or overdue (checked when you open the app).

You only see your own notifications. There are no emails.

11. Reports

Menu → Reports. Use the same Date range and All / Mine controls as Home. Five reports, each with a table, a Total row and a Download CSV button:

  1. Pipeline by stage: open deals right now (ignores the date range).
  2. Lead sources and conversion: leads created in the range, by source, and how many converted.
  3. Sales performance by owner: deals won (count and value), lost, win rate.
  4. Quotations summary: quotations issued in the range, by status.
  5. Overdue tasks by assignee: open items due before today with a due date inside the range. For all overdue items, pick a wide custom range.

Reports and Home use the same calculations, so their numbers agree.

12. Global search

The Search… box sits in the top bar on every page.

  1. Click it, or press Ctrl+K (⌘K on Mac) or /.
  2. Type at least 2 characters. Results appear grouped: Leads, Contacts, Companies, Deals, Quotations (up to 5 each).
  3. Use the ↑ and ↓ keys and Enter, or click a result. If a group has more, click See all matching ….
  4. Esc closes the results. Press Esc again to clear the box.

Each word must match, so "asha rao" finds Asha Rao. Archived records are not shown.

13. Settings, Users, API keys and Audit log

Menu → Settings. Staff see only My profile. Admins also see an Administration section.

  • Organisation (Admin only): display name, legal name, GSTIN, address, phone (same rules as section 5), email, website. These print on quotation PDFs. Currency (INR) and time zone (Asia/Kolkata) are shown but cannot be changed.
  • Lead sources (Admin only): Add source, Rename, Deactivate / Reactivate, reorder with the arrows. "Website", "Manual" and "Import" are System sources and cannot be changed. A deactivated source stays on old leads but is no longer offered.
  • Pipeline stages (Admin only): Add stage, Edit (name and probability), reorder open stages, Delete (only for a stage no deal has ever used). Won (100%) and Lost (0%) are always last. New deals start in the first open stage.
  • Users (Admin only): see section 3.
  • API keys (Admin only): New API key → name → Create key. The full key is shown once only, so copy it then. Rotate issues a new key and stops the old one. Revoke stops it for good.
  • Audit log (Admin only): a list of important actions (users, deactivations, archives, conversions, deal outcomes, quotations, settings, imports, API keys) with When, Actor, Action, Record, Details. Filter by action, record type, person and date. It shows field names, never passwords or keys.

14. Good to know

  • The first click can be slow. The test (staging) setup is deployed: the web app on Vercel, the API on Render, the database on Supabase, all on free plans. The API sleeps after about 15 minutes without use, and the first request afterwards can take up to a minute. After that it runs at normal speed. The free database can also pause after a long idle period, and someone must un-pause it from its dashboard.
  • Fictional data only on staging and in demos. Never enter real customer details.
  • Nothing is deleted. Leads, companies, contacts, deals, quotations and form submissions are archived and can be restored.
  • Not in this version (agreed or reported, not forgotten):
    • no "forgot password" email; an Admin resets passwords
    • no emails of any kind (notifications are in-app only)
    • no product or service catalogue; quotation lines are typed in
    • a GST rate per line (any rate, 0–100), but no CGST/SGST/IGST split
    • no Excel (.xlsx) import; save as CSV instead
    • no custom fields, no saved filters (bookmark the filtered web address instead)
    • no PDF reports; reports download as CSV
    • no notification settings, and no notifications for deal stage changes or Won/Lost
    • no bulk actions on lists, no calendar view, no read/unread on form submissions
    • currency and time zone are fixed (INR, India time)

15. Likely evaluator questions

How do roles and permissions work?

There are two roles, Admin and Staff. Each action needs a permission (for example "leads:write"). The server checks it on every request. Hiding a button is only for convenience: if Staff tried an Admin action directly, the server would refuse it.

Can Staff see each other's records?

Yes. Sales records are shared across the team. "Owner" records who is responsible. Mine filters let each person focus on their own.

How does login work?

Email and password. Passwords are stored only as a scrambled one-way hash. After sign-in the browser holds a random session code. The server keeps the session, so it can end it instantly. Deactivating a user or changing a password signs that person out everywhere.

What stops password guessing?

After several failed attempts for the same email, further attempts are refused for a while ("Too many attempts. Try again later.").

How does the website form reach the CRM?

The website's server sends each form submission to a private CRM address, with an API key created on the API keys page. Browsers never see the key. It has been live since 25 November 2026.

What if the CRM is down when someone submits the website form?

The website still works. It keeps saving, emailing and sending enquiries to HubSpot as before, so the submission is not lost. If the same submission reaches the CRM twice, the CRM recognises its submission ID and ignores the repeat.

How are quotation totals calculated?

Each line: quantity × unit price, less the line's discount, then the line's own GST rate. Tax exclusive: GST is added on top. Tax inclusive: the entered price already contains the GST, which is split out of it. Subtotal = sum of the lines before GST; one GST row per rate used; Total = sum of the lines. Rounding to the paisa (half-up) happens once per line for each amount, so the printed figures always add up.

Why are the totals exact?

Money is calculated with exact decimal arithmetic, not computer floating-point, which can drift by a paisa. The server always recalculates the totals itself, and the screen, the list and the PDF show the same figure.

How are duplicates handled?

When you add a lead or company by hand, a matching email, phone or name shows a warning, but you can still save. CSV imports skip rows whose email already exists, and list them. A repeated website submission is ignored. Conversion suggests existing companies and contacts so you can link them instead of creating copies.

What happens when a lead is converted?

In one step, the app links or creates the company and contact, can start a deal, and marks the lead Converted. If any part fails, nothing is saved. The lead and its history are kept.

What is audited?

Creating, editing, archiving and restoring records; lead status changes and conversions; deal stage moves and Won/Lost; quotation changes; user, role and password changes; settings; API keys; imports; and the outcome of each website enquiry. The log entry is saved together with the change, so a change can't happen without one.

Can data be deleted?

Business records are archived, never deleted. The one exception: an Admin can delete a pipeline stage that no deal has ever used.

Do the dashboard numbers match the data?

Yes. Automated tests compare every dashboard number with an independent database count, and check that Reports agree with Home.

Why were some PRD items left out?

To finish the full core journey reliably in the time available. Each item left out is recorded with a reason. Some were approved by the project owner, and some are awaiting a decision (section 17).

How was it tested?

841 automated tests run against a real database. Step-by-step manual test scripts exist for every feature. Running them in a browser is the next step.

16. Technical overview

Three parts

PartWhat it isWhere it runs (staging)
Web appThe screens (Next.js, React, TypeScript)Vercel (free plan)
APIBusiness rules and permissions (Express, TypeScript)Render (free plan)
DatabasePostgreSQL 17, in its own separate areaSupabase (free plan)

The browser talks only to the web app, which passes requests to the API. Only the API talks to the database. The CRM's data lives in its own database area with its own login, separate from the website's data. For local use the whole app also starts with one Docker command.

Login and sessions

  • Email and password. Passwords are hashed with Argon2.
  • On sign-in the server creates a random session token. The browser stores it in a cookie that page scripts cannot read; the database stores only a hash of it.
  • Sessions last 7 days and are extended while the app is in use. Logout, deactivation or a password change ends them immediately.
  • Two roles (Admin, Staff) map to 19 named permissions, checked by the API on every route.

Security measures

  • Permission check on every API route, and every query is limited to the user's organisation.
  • Input checked against one shared set of validation rules.
  • Rate limits on login and on website intake (per key and per address).
  • Same-site request check against cross-site attacks; standard security headers.
  • API keys stored only as hashes and shown once. They can be revoked or rotated.
  • Website intake: idempotency (no duplicates on retry), a hidden bot trap, and size and type limits.
  • CSV exports are protected against spreadsheet formula injection.
  • Errors give a reference ID, never internal details. The audit log shows only approved fields.
  • Related changes are saved together in one database transaction, including their audit row.

Free-tier hosting

Everything runs on free plans with no paid dependency. The trade-offs: the API sleeps after about 15 minutes idle (up to a minute to wake), the database can pause when unused for long, and connections are limited. This is fine for evaluation, not for production.

Before the demo: have sample data loaded, and submit the website's contact form once with fictional details. It also goes through the website's normal emails and HubSpot, so tell the team it is a test. Allow a minute for the first page to wake up.

  2. Lead. On the submission, click the linked lead. Its source is Website and the original message is under Original enquiry.
  3. Work the lead. Open the Timeline tab, add a Call ("Intro call"), then click Qualified under Change status.
  4. Convert. Click Convert, keep Create new for company and contact, leave Create a deal on, enter a Value (₹), click Convert lead.

  6. Quotation. Open Quotations → New quotation. Pick the new company, contact and deal. Add two lines and watch the totals update. Click Create quotation.
  7. PDF. Click Download PDF and open it.
  8. Send and accept. Click Mark as sent, then Accepted → Mark accepted.
