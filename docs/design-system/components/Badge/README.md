# Badge

A short status label (one or two words) with a dot, used in tables, cards and record headers.

Colour always carries meaning, and the word always says the same thing — never colour alone.

| Tone | Sales & CRM | Projects | HR & People |
|---|---|---|---|
| `success` | Won, Paid | Completed, On track | Active, Approved |
| `warning` | Follow-up due | At risk, Due soon | Pending approval, Probation |
| `danger` | Lost, Overdue invoice | Overdue, Blocked | Rejected, Exited |
| `info` | Proposal sent | In review | Interview scheduled |
| `accent` | New lead | New | New joiner |
| neutral | Draft | Not started | Inactive |

Keep the mapping identical across all three products so users learn it once.
